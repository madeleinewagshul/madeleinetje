Madeleine Wagshul is a Canadian-American artist and researcher currently located in Miami, Florida. Her artistic and academic work is interdisciplinary, focused on combining the visual arts, anthropology, geography, and ecology through storytelling.



Although Madeleine primarily works with illustration and animation, she frequently engages in costume design, sculpture, film, and photography. She includes her original illustrations in her academic research and is interested in depicting organic form through visceral imagery.



In her free time, Madeleine loves to hike, read, browse Google Earth, and spend time with her cat. She is currently obtaining a BFA in art and technology from the University of Florida and will be pursuing geography at a graduate level.
